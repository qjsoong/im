# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This project is a place to build an input method, not a
place to win arguments.

## What that means here

Participants are expected to:

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and ask before concluding
  otherwise.
- Critique the code, the design and the data, not the person who wrote them.
- Accept that a maintainer may close a discussion with "not now" — a decision
  about a patch is not a judgement about its author.
- Respect that this project is written and documented in English so that it can
  be read by anyone, and that contributors may be writing in a second or third
  language. Do not correct someone's grammar in review unless a comment is
  genuinely unclear.

The following are not acceptable:

- Insults, harassment, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Bad-faith argumentation: moving goalposts, argumentum ad hominem, or relitigating
  a settled decision in an unrelated thread.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else the
project is being discussed.

## Scope and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or ban
a participant, with or without explanation, when behaviour makes the project
worse for everyone else.

If you are subject to or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
the maintainers through GitHub, or by email to the address in the git history of
this repository. Reports will be handled discreetly.
